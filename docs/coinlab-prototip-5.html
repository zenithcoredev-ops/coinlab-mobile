💎 Diamond Tournament

Finish that week in the top 8 of Diamond League and you enter the tournament. Each stage lasts 1 week: Quarterfinal (8→6) → Semifinal (6→4) → Final (ranked among 4). Only the 4 finalists win a prize.

🎖️ QuarterfinalTop 6 advance
🥉 SemifinalTop 4 advance
🥈 Final — 2nd$25,000
🥉 Final — 3rd$10,000
🏆 Final — 1st (Champion)$50,000

4th place in the Final wins nothing — so reaching the Final isn't enough, the competition stays alive until the end. A limited number of global Diamond brackets run each season, funded by platform-wide ad and subscription revenue — not one prize pool per Diamond group. League ranking is always earned purely through effort — mining, tasks, streak, and steps — never through purchases.
